## P2-C1 — Fixed-step vehicle simulation

State: done. Requires P1 (`ae25a13`).

Work: `FixedStepper` (60 Hz, bounded catch-up with reported dropped time,
interpolation alpha). Validated `CarDefinition` (`public/assets/cars/fr26.json`,
3.4 m wheelbase, 800 kg with driver). `createVehicleSimulation` builds a Rapier rigid
chassis with explicit mass properties and a four-wheel raycast vehicle controller;
consumers see `DriverControls` in and plain `VehicleSnapshot`s out. Reset,
disposal and `PHYSICS_VERSION` (`p2.1`). Shared content validators in
`src/content/validate.ts`.

Rapier facts found: `brake` is a per-step rolling-friction impulse cap (converted
from newtons × dt) and is ignored while a wheel has engine force; `frictionSlip`
behaves as the tyre friction coefficient; the forward-axis setter is the property
`setIndexForwardAxis`.

Evidence: 67 unit tests pass: stepping at 30/60/75/144/240 Hz gives 60 steps/s,
bounded catch-up, rest on four wheels (< 5 cm drift in 5 s), 0–100 km/h in
1.6–3.2 s (measured 2.05 s), bounded braking from 200 km/h, steering sign, control
clamping, reset, identical repeated trajectories and **bit-identical state at 30,
60 and 144 Hz render rates**. No drag yet, so top speed is unbounded until P2-C3.

## P2-C2 — Controls, assists, gears, camera and greybox track

State: in progress. Built test-first (red → green vertical slices), one seam at a time.

Test seams: `parseTrack`/`fetchTrack` (content boundary); `buildTrackGeometry` →
`pointAt`/`locate` (lap distance, signed lateral offset, surface); the shipped circuit's
layout requirements from [P4](../phases/P4-content.md); keyboard adapter (DOM events in,
`DriverControls` out); the driver-aid pipeline (raw input + snapshot in, assisted
controls out); the automatic gearbox (speed/throttle in, gear/rpm out); the chase camera
rig (snapshot in, camera pose out); the game session through the dev test hooks; and
browser checks (keys move the car, focus loss clears input and pauses).

Track: Harbour Park, 3.93 km, 12 m wide with 1.5 m kerbs, clockwise, with a 1.29 km
back straight, R18 hairpin, 90° R250 sweeper, 35° chicane and medium corners.
Tests assert those features, ≥ 25 m clearance between non-adjacent parts, and that the
start is on the road. Mutating the layout (soft chicane, wide hairpin) or the geometry
(lateral sign, stale-hint fallback) makes them fail.
