## P2-C1 — Fixed-step vehicle simulation

State: done. Requires P1 (`ae25a13`).

Work: `FixedStepper` (60 Hz, bounded catch-up with reported dropped time,
interpolation alpha). Validated `CarDefinition` (`public/assets/cars/fr26.json`,
3.4 m wheelbase, 800 kg with driver). `createVehicleSimulation` builds a Rapier rigid
chassis with explicit mass properties and a four-wheel raycast vehicle controller;
consumers see `DriverControls` in and plain `VehicleSnapshot`s out. Reset,
disposal and `PHYSICS_VERSION` (`p2.1`). Shared content validators in
`src/content/validate.ts`.

Rapier facts found: `brake` is a per-step rolling-friction impulse cap (converted
from newtons × dt) and is ignored while a wheel has engine force; `frictionSlip`
behaves as the tyre friction coefficient; the forward-axis setter is the property
`setIndexForwardAxis`.

Evidence: 67 unit tests pass: stepping at 30/60/75/144/240 Hz gives 60 steps/s,
bounded catch-up, rest on four wheels (< 5 cm drift in 5 s), 0–100 km/h in
1.6–3.2 s (measured 2.05 s), bounded braking from 200 km/h, steering sign, control
clamping, reset, identical repeated trajectories and **bit-identical state at 30,
60 and 144 Hz render rates**. No drag yet, so top speed is unbounded until P2-C3.

## P2-C2 — Controls, assists, gears, camera and greybox track

State: in progress. Built test-first (red → green vertical slices), one seam at a time.

Test seams: `parseTrack`/`fetchTrack` (content boundary); `buildTrackGeometry` →
`pointAt`/`locate` (lap distance, signed lateral offset, surface); the shipped circuit's
layout requirements from [P4](../phases/P4-content.md); keyboard adapter (DOM events in,
`DriverControls` out); the driver-aid pipeline (raw input + snapshot in, assisted
controls out); the automatic gearbox (speed/throttle in, gear/rpm out); the chase camera
rig (snapshot in, camera pose out); the game session through the dev test hooks; and
browser checks (keys move the car, focus loss clears input and pauses).

Track: Harbour Park, 3.93 km, 12 m wide with 1.5 m kerbs, clockwise, with a 1.29 km
back straight, R18 hairpin, 90° R250 sweeper, 35° chicane and medium corners.
Tests assert those features, ≥ 25 m clearance between non-adjacent parts, and that the
start is on the road. Mutating the layout (soft chicane, wide hairpin) or the geometry
(lateral sign, stale-hint fallback) makes them fail.

Keyboard: arrows and WASD drive; R/C/Escape fire once per press (auto-repeat ignored);
game keys suppress page scrolling; window blur and a hidden tab release every key.

Assists (vehicle adapter, per wheel, from the previous step's load and cornering force).
Rapier halves forward impulse in its friction-circle test, so the car can brake at
about 3 g on μ 1.8 and its wheels cannot lock or spin. Assists therefore limit demand
against the grip budget rather than modulating wheel slip:

- ABS caps each wheel's brake force at 90% of its remaining longitudinal budget. Braking
  hard at full lock from 150 km/h: peak sideslip > 8° without, < 4° with; straight-line
  stopping from 200 km/h stays within 15% (a 50% margin fails that test).
- Traction control applies the same cap to rear drive force. On μ 0.5 (grass-like),
  flooring it out of a 20 km/h corner: peak rear-axle slip 5.4° without, 1.2° with. On
  the shipped μ 1.8 the rear barely slides, so TC seldom intervenes until P2-C3 surfaces.
- Steering assist caps the front angle at L·μ·g/v² + 0.015 rad (peak slip from fixed-lock
  sweeps: best lock ≈ 4° at 100 km/h, 2° at 180, ≤ 1° at 250). Full lock at 250 km/h
  turns further and keeps more speed; low-speed lock is untouched.

Keyboard smoothing (`createInputSmoother`): steering ramps to full lock in 0.25 s and
re-centres twice as fast; held-key lock is 1/√(1 + (v/30 m/s)²): 73% at 100 km/h, 40%
at 250 km/h. Pedals stay on/off; traction control and ABS handle the rest.

Gearbox (`createGearbox`, data in `fr26.json` `powertrain.gearbox`): eight gears topping
out at 95–345 km/h at a 12 500 rpm redline; upshift 11 800, downshift 8 000 rpm. Content
validation rejects gears that would land below the downshift point after an upshift, so
the box cannot hunt. One shift per update. Drive force is still power-limited, so gears
feed the HUD (and later audio) but do not yet change acceleration; P2-C3's powertrain
module can consume them.

Camera rig (`src/rendering/camera-rig.ts`, no Three.js): the chase view tracks the car's
position exactly, 6 m behind and 2.2 m up, and follows its heading with a 0.3 s
exponential lag, so turns read as the car rotating in frame; the lag decays per elapsed
second, so 30 and 144 fps give the same path. C cycles to a rigid nose camera; reset
drops the lag.
