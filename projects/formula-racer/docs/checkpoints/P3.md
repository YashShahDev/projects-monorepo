## P3-C1 — Laps, checkpoints and timing

State: done. Requires P2 (`0006df7`).

Lap timer (`src/simulation/lap-timer.ts`, pure): progress is the running sum of each
step's change in lap distance, wrapped at the loop's seam, so reversing subtracts.
Three sector boundaries at equal fractions must be reached in order, driving forward,
before the line closes a lap. Crossing times are interpolated within the step (a
3000 m lap at 50 m/s times at exactly 60 s). A jump of more than 30 m between updates
means the nearest centreline point moved elsewhere, i.e. a shortcut: it earns no
distance and invalidates the lap, so the skipped stretch must be driven again. Backing
over the line never counts.

Session: a 3 s standing-start countdown (counted in whole steps) holds the car on the
brakes, and timing starts at GO. Track limits follow the four-wheels rule: the lap stays
valid until the inner wheels (half-track + 0.2 m) are past the kerb. The stricter
centre-on-grass rule invalidated a lap that ran 7.52 m wide at the hairpin with two
wheels still on the kerb. Invalid laps are still timed. Reset abandons the lap and
restarts the countdown. Completed laps carry the physics version, assists and tuning.
HUD: countdown overlay, running lap clock (red when invalid) and last lap with ✕ when
invalid; times truncate to m:ss.mmm like a timing screen.

Test support: `tests/support/autopilot.ts` (moved to `src/app/autopilot.ts` in P5-C1), a keyboard-only driver, completes clean
Harbour Park laps (≈111 s, max 7.3 m from the centreline). This proves the layout is
drivable end to end and gives later phases a full-lap fixture.

Evidence: 154 unit tests; `make test-full` 38/38. Mutation checks: removing the
shortcut guard fails its test.

## P3-C2 — Energy rules, deployment and active aero

State: done.

Regulation selected and deviations recorded in [P3](../phases/P3-time-trial.md) and
[RES-002](../research/RES-002-energy-rules.md): Section C Issue 18 and Section B Issue 07.
`public/assets/rules/energy-2026-c18.json` names its source; `permittedDeployW` follows
C5.2.8(i) (350 kW to 290 km/h, 300 kW at 300 km/h, 60 kW at 342 km/h, zero from
345 km/h). The pure energy system tracks the 4 MJ window from full, the 8.5 MJ per-lap
Recharge, the 50 km/h standing-start rule, Balanced/Shift/Harvest deployment, and
regeneration blended ahead of friction braking. A 3000-step mixed sequence conserves
energy to the millijoule.

Vehicle integration (physics `p3.1`): the car's `maxPowerW` is now the ICE alone
(400 kW power curve, still under the 11 kN traction cap); ERS deployment adds on top,
limited by the C5.2.8 curve, the battery and the C5.2.11 MGU-K torque of 500 Nm at the
crankshaft. An earlier draft limited deployment by the traction cap instead, which
blocked nearly all of it below ~245 km/h. Rapier drops engine force on a braking wheel,
so braking also stops deployment. Regeneration is accounted from requested braking
power (brake force × speed) and does not change the brake force: stopping from 250 to
60 km/h matches the ICE-only car to within 1.5 m. Snapshots carry mode, charge,
deploy/regen power, lap Recharge and wing state; completing a lap starts a new Recharge
allowance.

Keys: held Shift requests full deployment; E cycles Balanced ↔ Harvest. HUD: mode,
charge %, net ERS kW and wing mode.

Active aero: tracks define `activeAeroZones` by lap distance (Harbour Park: 40–260 m and
2380–3450 m, inside the two straights). The car's `aero.straightMode` (CdA 0.95, ClA 1.6)
must shed drag (content validation). Wings move over `wingTransitionS` (0.4 s,
C3.10.10 o). The session requests Straight Mode only in a zone on throttle and off the
brakes; the vehicle forces Corner Mode whenever braking. Straight Mode keeps a coasting
car > 5 km/h faster over 3 s from 250 km/h.

Found while testing:

- Browser tests had slowed to 25–30 s each, near the 30 s timeout, because the 3 s
  countdown made every driving test render ~180 more frames. Measured with one worker in
  headless SwiftShader: 30.6 ms per frame at 1280×720, 14.7 ms at 640×360. The test
  viewport is now 640×360; the Chromium dev suite went from 44 s to 15 s.
- At 640×360 the road vanished: grass 2 cm below the road z-fought and won everywhere.
  This was confirmed by lowering the grass (road reappears) and fixed with polygon offset
  (grass behind, start line in front). It would have shown as distant flicker on
  hardware.

Evidence: 193 unit tests; `make test-full` 43/43.

## P3-C3 — HUD, session menu and persistence

State: done.

Lap store (`src/app/lap-store.ts`): best laps keyed by track id, physics version and
assist set (`harbour|p3.1|SAT`), saved under one versioned `localStorage` entry. Invalid
and tuned laps never become bests. Storage that throws on access (privacy mode, blocked
site data) or on write (quota) falls back to memory for the session and says so. Corrupt
JSON, another schema version or malformed entries are discarded whole, reported, and
replaced on the next best.

Menu: Escape opens a Paused dialog with Resume, Restart and assist checkboxes. Changing
an assist restarts on the grid, so a lap is always driven under one assist set. The
keyboard now ignores keys only in text inputs; a focused checkbox or button still passes
Escape. HUD adds the best lap for the current assist set and a storage notice.

Browser coverage: menu resume; assist change restarting on the grid; a pre-saved best
shown after load; corrupt saved data and blocked storage both still start the game;
running wide marks the lap invalid.

Evidence: 203 unit tests; `make test-full` 73/73.

## Phase review

Reviewer: Codex, model `gpt-6-astra` (codex-companion `review --base 09c6ec9^ --scope
branch`), 2026-09-26. Three findings, all reproduced with a failing test first,
accepted and fixed (physics `p3.2`):

1. Harvest created free energy: lift-off regeneration stored 216 kJ over 2 s while the car
   coasted exactly like Balanced, and it charged at rest. The energy system now reports the
   lift-off share (`engineBrakeW`), the vehicle applies it as drivetrain braking (P/v), and
   harvesting stops below 5 m/s. On a near-drag-free car the extra kinetic energy lost now
   exceeds the energy stored. (Comparing normal cars understated it, because the slower
   Harvest car also loses less to drag.)
2. After a shortcut, every later lap boundary was displaced by the skipped distance
   (the next valid lap ended 800 m past the line). The jump now counts as distance, so
   boundaries stay on the real start line, and the lap it happens in is still invalid.
   This replaces the earlier rule that the skipped stretch had to be driven again.
3. The storage notice was computed once at startup, so a save failing later (quota) left
   it hidden. `storageNotice(status)` is recomputed every frame. Unit-tested; browser
   coverage would need a completed lap (~100 s of simulation), so that path is not
   exercised in the browser.

After the fixes: 208 unit tests; `make test-full` 73/73.
