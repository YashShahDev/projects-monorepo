# Phase 6 checkpoint record

Phase: [P6](../phases/P6-polish.md). Requested by the user after playing P5; the work
lands in PR #7.

## P6-C1 — Gearbox modes and reverse

State: done. Physics moved from p3.6 to p3.8.

- `X` shifts up and `Z` shifts down. The shift is applied on the next simulation
  step, and ignored during the countdown and while paused. A restart clears it.
- **Automatic**: upshifts where the next gear gives more wheel force at the same road
  speed. The widely spaced low gears hold to the redline (1→2 at 12,467 rpm), and the
  top gears shift at the crossover (6→7 at 11,412 rpm and 7→8 at 11,424 rpm), where
  the old fixed point was 11,800 rpm.
- **Hybrid**: the driver's shifts are taken. The box itself upshifts only at the
  limiter and downshifts only below half the downshift point. An upshift that would
  bog the engine is refused, so the box never undoes a driver's shift.
- **Manual**: only the keys shift. A downshift that would over-rev is refused, and the
  limiter cuts drive, restoring it at 97% of the redline.
- **Reverse**: `Z` at a standstill selects R. It drives backwards up to a 30 km/h
  limiter, with no ERS. Brakes stop the car without driving it forwards.
- Traction control now limits the size of the force in both directions.
- Every shift cuts drive for the shift time, downshifts included.
- The Pause menu has a Gearbox choice, and it is saved. Changing it restarts the lap,
  and best laps are kept per mode; Automatic keeps its old key. The gear readout shows
  `R`, and `H` or `M` next to the gear.
- One test was restated rather than kept: "drive rises after an upshift" encoded the
  old fixed shift point. At the crossover the force is equal, so the test now checks
  that no drive is lost.
- Gates: `make lint` passes; `make test` 392 unit tests and 32/32 Chromium browser
  tests.

The car's gearing is not yet retuned to the 2026 figures (a 13,000 rpm redline, 8th at
about 360 km/h). That changes performance, so it moves to P6-C5, together with the
measured baseline.

## P6-C2 — Pause menu redesign and keyboard help

State: done.

- The pause menu is now a card with an accent bar. It has Resume, Restart and Controls
  buttons, and sections for Driving, Assists, Display and Audio. The assists and sound
  are switch toggles (still real checkboxes, so keyboard and screen readers work the
  same), with styled selects and keycap hints.
- A **Controls** overlay opens with `H`, with `?` (matched by character, so it works
  on any keyboard layout), or from the menu's Controls button.
  - It is generated from the keyboard's own binding tables (`CONTROLS_HELP`), so it
    cannot drift from the real keys.
  - It pauses the game and owns the keyboard while open: `Esc` or `H` close only the
    help, and focus returns to what had it.
  - `help` is not a session action. The session's camera fallback now carries a
    `satisfies "camera"` check, which caught exactly that fall-through at compile time.
- Tests:
  - keyboard: H and `?` give `help`; the help covers every binding once;
  - browser: H opens the help over the pause menu, Esc closes only the help, and the
    Controls button round-trips focus;
  - the existing menu tests still pass against the new markup.
- Gates: `make lint` passes; `make test` 394 unit tests and 34/34 Chromium browser
  tests.

## P6-C3 — Bigger corner preview

State: done.

- The preview now looks 600 m ahead (it used to show one corner) and names the corner
  after the next one: "then Turn 2 · Left · 290 m". Both corners carry their numbers
  on the drawn road, at their apexes.
- The road is scaled to fit the box, with the car kept at the bottom centre. The scale
  is set by the road ahead only: the short stretch behind the car is drawn but never
  shrinks the view.
- The box scales with the screen's short side (`clamp(96px, 28vmin, 200px)`): 200 px
  at 720p, and about 100 px at 640×360, where a fixed 200 px box covered the car. The
  car-pixel smoke tests caught that.
- Tests:
  - `nextCorners` lists the corners in the order the car meets them, wraps past the last
    corner, and never repeats one;
  - the fitted preview stays in the box around the whole lap;
  - `at()` places a lap distance on the drawn road;
  - browser: both labels and the numbers show, and the box is at least 190 px wide at
    1280×720 and under a third of the height at 640×360.
- Gates: `make lint` passes; `make test` 399 unit tests and 34/34 Chromium browser
  tests.
