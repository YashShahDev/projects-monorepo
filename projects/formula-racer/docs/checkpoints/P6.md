# Phase 6 checkpoint record

Phase: [P6](../phases/P6-polish.md). Requested by the user after playing P5; the work
lands in PR #7.

## P6-C1 — Gearbox modes and reverse

State: done. Physics moved from p3.6 to p3.8.

- `X` shifts up and `Z` shifts down. The shift is applied on the next simulation
  step, and ignored during the countdown and while paused. A restart clears it.
- **Automatic**: upshifts where the next gear gives more wheel force at the same road
  speed. The widely spaced low gears hold to the redline (1→2 at 12,467 rpm), and the
  top gears shift at the crossover (6→7 at 11,412 rpm and 7→8 at 11,424 rpm), where
  the old fixed point was 11,800 rpm.
- **Hybrid**: the driver's shifts are taken. The box itself upshifts only at the
  limiter and downshifts only below half the downshift point. An upshift that would
  bog the engine is refused, so the box never undoes a driver's shift.
- **Manual**: only the keys shift. A downshift that would over-rev is refused, and the
  limiter cuts drive, restoring it at 97% of the redline.
- **Reverse**: `Z` at a standstill selects R. It drives backwards up to a 30 km/h
  limiter, with no ERS. Brakes stop the car without driving it forwards.
- Traction control now limits the size of the force in both directions.
- Every shift cuts drive for the shift time, downshifts included.
- The Pause menu has a Gearbox choice, and it is saved. Changing it restarts the lap,
  and best laps are kept per mode; Automatic keeps its old key. The gear readout shows
  `R`, and `H` or `M` next to the gear.
- One test was restated rather than kept: "drive rises after an upshift" encoded the
  old fixed shift point. At the crossover the force is equal, so the test now checks
  that no drive is lost.
- Gates: `make lint` passes; `make test` 392 unit tests and 32/32 Chromium browser
  tests.

The car's gearing is not yet retuned to the 2026 figures (a 13,000 rpm redline, 8th at
about 360 km/h). That changes performance, so it moves to P6-C5, together with the
measured baseline.

## P6-C5 — Physics and assists: measured, then fixed

State: done. Physics version `p3.9`.

`bun run tools/characterize.ts` measures the envelope: acceleration, braking, top
speed, steady cornering g both ways, and sideslip under power. Each figure is taken on
grip 1.0 and 0.5, with the assists on and off.

**Root cause.** Rapier's raycast vehicle halves the forward impulse in its
friction-circle test (Bullet's `fwdFactor`). So every tyre could brake and drive with
twice its cornering grip, and keep most of its cornering grip while doing so. The ABS
and TC budget reproduced the doubling on purpose. The effects:

- braking at 100 km/h was about 3 g;
- the launch was 1.4 g;
- a locked or spinning wheel lost nothing, so ABS stopped _longer_ than locked wheels
  and TC off gave no power oversteer.

**Fix.**

- The car applies the true friction circle: it caps each wheel's lengthways force and
  sets Rapier's `frictionSlip` to `√(μ² − 0.75·(L/N)²)`, which turns Rapier's
  `(L/2)² + S² ≤ (slip·N)²` into `L² + S² ≤ (μN)²`.
- A wheel asked for more than its budget slides at 0.75 of peak grip until the demand
  drops to what a sliding tyre carries.
  - A spinning rear wheel stays in Rapier's circle at sliding grip.
  - A locked wheel's friction is applied directly, against its contact patch's velocity.
    Rapier would push along the wheel's rolling direction, and a locked, steered front
    would still steer.
- ABS and TC keep each tyre's total use within 90% of its grip. ABS holds the rear to
  70%, as brake distribution does: with the true circle, a light rear braking at 90%
  spun the car when braking into a turn.
- Gearing, from the P6 research: redline 13,000 rpm; 8th geared to 360 km/h (tops 99,
  136, 172, 209, 245, 282, 318, 360); power curve extended to 13,000 rpm; shift
  fallbacks at 12,300 and 8,300 rpm.

**Before and after** (grip 1, all assists on, no ERS, flat ground):

| Measure              | Target (public F1 figures) | p3.8   | p3.9   |
| -------------------- | -------------------------- | ------ | ------ |
| 0–100 km/h           | about 2.6 s                | 1.62 s | 2.38 s |
| 0–200 km/h           | 4.5–5.5 s                  | 3.50 s | 4.53 s |
| 100–0 km/h           | about 20 m                 | 12.8 m | 23.4 m |
| 200–0 km/h           | well under 4 × the 100–0   | 40.7 m | 69.5 m |
| Lateral g, 100 km/h  | about 2                    | 1.84   | 1.84   |
| Lateral g, 200 km/h  | 3–4                        | 3.31   | 3.24   |
| Straight braking yaw | under 1°                   | 0.00°  | 0.00°  |
| Left vs right        | within 2%                  | equal  | equal  |

With the assists off:

- locked wheels stop 8% longer than ABS;
- locked brakes drift 1–2° off the heading;
- flooring it out of a 60 km/h turn spins the car, as it does a real one with no TC.

**Tests** (`tests/vehicle-envelope.test.ts`, through `createVehicleSimulation`):

- launch and braking bands;
- a 200 km/h stop well under four times the 100 km/h stop;
- ABS beats locked wheels on grip 1 and 0.5;
- locked wheels slide straight on while ABS steers;
- TC off slides the rear on low grip, TC on halves that;
- left/right mirror within 2%.

**Changed tests, and why:**

- `assists.test.ts` asserted that without ABS the rear slides (> 8° sideslip) when
  braking while turning. That came from the doubled circle: locked, steered fronts kept
  steering. Now every wheel locks and the car ploughs straight on. The test now asserts
  that ABS turns the car at least three times as far, with the rear still planted.
- Two worked examples (a drive-force figure and a parser bound) were recomputed for
  the new gearing.

**Limitations.**

- The tool's lateral-g sweep starts at a steer already past the limit on low grip at
  200 km/h, so it reports 0 there. That is a flaw in the measurement, not the car.
- Keyboard throttle is on/off, so with TC off, slow corners need throttle taps.

Gates: `make lint` passes; `make test` 411 unit tests and 34/34 Chromium browser tests.

## P6-C6 — Racing line and braking guide

State: done.

- **`buildRacingLine(geometry, limits)`** (`src/simulation/racing-line.ts`):
  - Returns a lateral offset per centreline sample that minimises the summed squared
    second difference of the line's points, which is its squared curvature. The line
    keeps the car's half width (half track plus half a tyre) and 0.3 m from each edge.
  - Solved by projected Gauss–Seidel. Each coordinate's step is exact (a parabola of
    curvature 6), clamped to the band.
  - Coarse to fine, at 16 m, 4 m and then 2 m spacing, with the sweeps bounded
    (3,000, 800, 300). Long, smooth parts of the line hardly move on the fine grid
    alone.
  - Harbour builds in about 140 ms. The app builds the line once after the first frame,
    during the countdown.
- **Speed profile:**
  - Each point's grip limit comes from the line's curvature, with downforce growing
    with speed.
  - A forward pass (power, drive cap, drag) and a backward pass (brakes, drag) run on a
    friction circle. Both start at the slowest corner, where the profile must equal
    its limit, so the lap seam needs no extra iteration.
  - It plans on 0.8 of the tyre's quoted friction, so every corner speed stays under
    what the car held in C5.
- **Phases:** "brake" where the profile slows, "lift" for the 0.6 s before each braking
  zone, "go" elsewhere. `guideColour` raises a point to brake (or lift) when the car,
  at its current speed, must brake now (or within 0.6 s) to make that point's speed.
- **The ribbon** (`guide-view.ts`) is a 0.9 m unlit strip 3 cm above the road. Only
  the 300 m ahead of the car is drawn and recoloured each frame.
  - Menu: Racing line Off (default) / Braking zones / Full, saved with the other
    preferences.
  - The corner preview gains the speed to carry through the next corner (the slowest
    profile speed over it, rounded to 5 km/h).
- **Tests** (unit):
  - within the band everywhere and using it;
  - squared curvature under 0.7 of the centreline's;
  - continuous at the seam;
  - deterministic;
  - slowest at the hairpin;
  - slower through every grip-limited corner than on its approach;
  - never above C5's measured lateral g;
  - under 2 g of braking at low speed;
  - phases;
  - `guideColour` cases;
  - the corner speed hint across the lap seam;
  - the preference.
- **Tests** (browser):
  - the line is off by default, draws about 300 m ahead in Full and less in Braking
    zones, and Off hides it and is saved;
  - the preview shows a km/h hint.
- **Changed from the plan:** "the profile falls before every corner" is true only of
  corners that limit speed. Harbour's 250 m sweeper is taken flat out while the car is
  still accelerating. So the test checks every corner that reaches the grip limit
  anywhere, and requires those to be at least half of all corners.
- Checked on screen (headless GPU): green down the straight; red into Turn 1 at
  223 km/h, where the target is 100.
- Gates: `make lint` passes; `make test` 426 unit tests and 36/36 Chromium browser
  tests.

## P6-C7 — Ghost and live delta

State: done.

- **Recording** (`src/simulation/ghost.ts`, driven by the session's fixed step):
  - 10 Hz samples of lap time, x, z, heading and progress.
  - An exact sample at each end of the lap. The finish pose is interpolated to the lap
    timer's crossing time inside the step, and the next lap starts from that same pose.
  - Progress follows the first-passage rule (only a new maximum counts).
  - At most 1,500 samples. Every completed `SessionLap` carries its ghost.
- **Playback:** the session's frame reports the lap time at the drawn pose (between
  the last two steps). The ghost is posed at that time, so it moves in step with the
  car. Heading takes the short way round.
- **Delta:** the lap time now, minus the ghost's time at the car's furthest progress
  this lap. Negative (green, "−") means ahead; positive (red, "+") means behind. It is
  hidden before any ghost exists and until the car has made progress on a new lap.
- **Storage** (`src/app/ghost-store.ts`):
  - One ghost per best-lap key (track, physics, assists, gearbox).
  - Stored as `"1"` + base64 of f32 x/z/heading/progress plus u16 centiseconds
    (18 B a sample).
  - Twelve kept, the oldest saved dropped first.
  - Saved only when its lap sets a best (so tuned and invalid laps never become
    ghosts), after the frame. It stays in memory if storage refuses it.
  - Decoding rejects malformed, oversized, over-150 s and other-format data.
- **Look:** a clone of the car sharing its geometry, one translucent blue material,
  coarsest level of detail only.
- Menu: Ghost Off / Best lap (default) / Last lap.
- **Layout:** the corner preview moved under the track map. At the centre-top it sat
  over the road ahead, and hid the ghost and the braking guide. The timing row shrinks
  on windows under 720 px so it doesn't run into the map.
- **Tests:**
  - ghost: 10 Hz plus exact ends; 60 against 120 Hz; first passage while reversing;
    sample cap; pose interpolation and holding; delta sign; round trip and size;
    malformed, other-version and over-cap data rejected;
  - store: reload; eviction and re-save order; other schema and bad JSON; refusing
    storage;
  - session: a lap's ghost spans the grid to the line, the next lap starts timed from
    the line; the frame's lap time sits between the last two steps;
  - preference;
  - browser (`@dev`, test loop, through a new `drive(seconds)` hook that uses the
    autopilot): no ghost on lap 1; after a lap it is visible with a negative delta (a
    flying lap beats the standing-start ghost); saved; Off hides it and the delta.
- Checked on screen: the ghost launches ahead as a translucent car after a restart,
  and the delta reads −2.14 on a flying lap.
- Gates: `make lint` passes; `make test` 440 unit tests and 37/37 Chromium browser
  tests.
